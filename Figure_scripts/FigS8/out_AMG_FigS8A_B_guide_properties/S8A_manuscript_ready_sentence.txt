Guide position was associated with within-gene relative guide effect primarily within the first approximately 95 bp of the coding sequence (estimated breakpoint = 95 bp; 95% gene-bootstrap CI, 80-160 bp; median within-gene Spearman rho = 0.21, IQR = -0.16 to 0.51), whereas little additional relationship was observed at positions >95 bp (median rho = 0.05, IQR = -0.21 to 0.25; Supplementary Fig. S8A).
